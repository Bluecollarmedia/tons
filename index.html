Siren Board

Tap a tone to start it looping — tap it again anytime to restart it from the beginning. Hold a tone down to rapid-fire it, over and over, crazy fast — release to stop it. Up to 3 tones can run at once; the oldest auto-stops to make room for a new one. Tap Share to send a clip straight into WhatsApp, or the ⬇ to download it. Use ⚙ Reorder to arrange the buttons however you like.
